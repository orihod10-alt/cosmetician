# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Discerning luxury beauty clients who already shop La Mer, Byredo, Chanel. Taste-led, not price-led. They arrive wanting to find a professional whose aesthetic matches their own, and book with confidence. Context: inspired by editorial content, influenced by premium brands. The job: discover a skilled cosmetician and book with frictionless trust.

## Product Purpose

Cosmetician connects premium clients with skilled beauty professionals through an appointment booking experience that carries the same curatorial authority as the brands they love. Success looks like a client finding a cosmetician whose work resonates with them, and completing a booking that felt inevitable rather than effortful.

## Brand Personality

Quiet luxury. Refined without trying. Voice is measured, confident, never promotional.

Three words: luxurious, elevated, refined.

Emotional goal: the visitor should leave feeling like they've been let into something curated and exclusive, not converted.

## References

- **Glossier**: approachable-yet-premium, clean minimalism, direct voice, skin-first
- **Byredo**: austere editorial restraint, silent luxury, extreme typographic discipline
- **NET-A-PORTER**: curatorial authority, photography as hero, stark contrast, fashion editorial framing

## Anti-references

- Standard spa aesthetics: teal, white, leaf icons, soft gradients, "wellness" clichés
- Generic booking-tool UIs: form-forward, step-counter headers, calendar-widget-as-hero
- Promotional design: discount badges, urgency banners, star ratings as primary content

## Design Principles

1. **Curation over volume.** Every element earns its place. What's removed signals taste as much as what's kept.
2. **The booking is the editorial.** Discovery should feel like reading a magazine feature, not filling out a form. The cosmetician's story and work come before the calendar.
3. **Trust through precision.** Typography, spacing, and photography do the persuasion work. Copy only confirms what the image already communicated.
4. **Silent luxury.** No badges, no discounts, no limited-time offers. Exclusivity is implied through restraint, never declared through promotion.
5. **The professional is the hero.** The cosmetician's work is the visual centerpiece. The product recedes; the talent leads.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Standard best-practices for contrast, keyboard navigation, and interactive element sizing. No specific additional requirements at this stage.
